## Description (2030/4000)
Favorium turns everyday kindness into something you can hold.

When you do a favor for someone in your circle, they thank you with a gem. Not points, not likes: a one-of-a-kind gem that leaves their collection and joins yours. Every gem remembers where it has been, so its journey grows each time it changes hands.

HOW IT WORKS
• Pair in person. Share a short code or QR with someone in front of you. Favors only flow between people who have actually met.
• Offer a favor. Walk the dog, help with a move, check on a kitten. They accept, and when it's done they confirm it.
• Receive a gem. They choose one of their gems to thank you with, and it becomes yours to keep or pass on.

EVERY GEM IS ONE OF A KIND
Each gem is born with its own shape, colors and character. As it travels from person to person it gains rank, and its journey shows everyone who has held it.

WATCH FAVORS FROM AFAR
The gems you start with are your originals, and they stay connected to you wherever they go. Long after one leaves your hands, you can follow it as it's passed from friend to friend of friend, each step a favor done for someone, often between people you'll never meet. Your kindness keeps traveling, and you get to watch.

STEP INSIDE
Press and hold a gem to dive into it: a living kaleidoscope drawn from that gem's own light. Tilt your phone to steer it.

SECRET MESSAGES
Generous helpers can engrave a short, unsigned message inside a gem before it moves on. Only whoever holds the gem can find it, by stepping inside and being still.

TITLES THAT MEAN SOMETHING
Help more different people and your title grows: Samaritan, Saint, Angel, Seraph, Bodhisattva. Titles are earned by confirmed help, never bought.

A GENTLE ECONOMY
Everyone starts with two gems. A gem can change hands once a day, so kindness keeps moving rather than piling up.

SAFE BY DESIGN
You only connect with people you've paired with face to face. You can report or block anyone, and reports are reviewed by a person.

Favorium is free, with no ads and no tracking.
